1. What the app is

The app is a companion for the Smart Greenhouse ESP32 controller. It displays the state reported by the controller and sends your commands to it over your local network. It can also run a built-in demonstration that is clearly labelled as simulated.

2. Using the app safely

  • The app can operate physical equipment: a window actuator, a fan, an irrigation pump and lighting. Before sending a command, make sure people, animals and objects are clear of moving parts and that operating the equipment is safe.
  • The window actuator and fan have no position or rotation feedback. The state shown in the app is the commanded state confirmed by the controller, not a measurement of what the mechanism actually did.
  • The app is not a safety device and must not be the only means of protecting crops, property or people. Do not rely on it for frost, overheating, flooding or fire protection.
  • Values labelled DEMO or Simulated are not real measurements.
  • Keep your pairing key private and use the app only on a Wi-Fi network you trust. Anyone with the key and access to that network can control the controller.
