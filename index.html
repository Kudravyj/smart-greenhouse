Help for the Smart Greenhouse app for iPhone and iPad

Contact us

What the app does

Smart Greenhouse monitors and controls a Smart Greenhouse ESP32 touchscreen controller over your local Wi-Fi network: readings, the window, fan, irrigation pump, lighting and automatic mode. The app talks directly to the controller; it does not need an account or an internet connection.

Connecting to your controller

  1. Connect the controller to Wi-Fi from its on-screen Wi-Fi settings.
  2. Join the same Wi-Fi network on your iPhone or iPad.
  3. In the app, open Settings → ESP32 Connection. Enter greenhouse-touch.local, or the IP address shown on the controller screen.
  4. Enter the 16-character pairing key from the controller's Wi-Fi screen and tap Save & connect.
  5. When iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network, tap Allow.

Trying the app without a controller

Open Settings and tap No controller yet? Try demo mode. The app then shows a simulated greenhouse, clearly labelled DEMO. To leave demo mode, enter your controller's host and pairing key and tap Save & connect.

Troubleshooting

  • The app says Offline. Check that the phone and the controller are on the same Wi-Fi network, and that Local Network access is enabled for Smart Greenhouse in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • greenhouse-touch.local does not resolve. Enter the IP address shown on the controller screen instead.
  • The controller cannot join Wi-Fi. After 30 seconds it starts a recovery hotspot named Greenhouse-…. Join it using the pairing key as the password and set the app host to 192.168.4.1.
  • Controls are greyed out. Controls are disabled while the controller is offline, while another command is pending, or when the controller reports a fault.
