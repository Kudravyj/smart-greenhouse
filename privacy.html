Privacy Policy

Smart Greenhouse for iOS · Effective 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the Smart Greenhouse app for iPhone and iPad ("the app") handles information. The app is provided by TGAGROCULTURE Sp. z o.o., Poland ("we", "us").

In short: the app does not collect, transmit to us, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. It communicates only with your own greenhouse controller on your local network.

Information stored on your device

To reconnect to your controller, the app saves on your device:

  • the controller's hostname or IP address, and
  • the controller's 16-character pairing key.

This information stays on your device, is not sent to us, and is removed when you delete the app.

Information exchanged with your controller

The app sends commands to, and receives readings and status from, the greenhouse controller you pair it with. This happens directly between your device and the controller over your local Wi-Fi network. If you use the app to change the controller's Wi-Fi network, the network name and password you enter are sent to the controller only; the app does not store the password.

Permissions

  • Local Network – needed to find and talk to the controller on your Wi-Fi network. It is not used for any other purpose.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, usage data, diagnostics or greenhouse readings. The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about users of the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you contact us by email, we use your message and address only to reply to you; you may ask us to delete that correspondence at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych).

Changes

If a future version of the app adds features that process personal data (for example optional accounts), we will update this policy and its effective date before those features are released.
